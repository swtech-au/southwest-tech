Southwest Tech is officially a regular thing. We're five-plus meetups in now, and every time there's a mix of familiar faces and new ones turning up, which is exactly what we hoped for.

Since we're meeting regularly, we've moved scheduling over to the [events page]({{ '/events/' | relative_url }}) and a shared [Lu.ma calendar]({{ site.luma.calendar_url }}). It's the easiest way to see what's coming up and when. The next few dates are already on there, and you can subscribe to the calendar so new meetups land straight in your own.

If you've been meaning to come along, it would be great to see you. Don't be a stranger. Arrive when you like, leave when you like, and talk to people about what you're building, breaking, or thinking about.

And if you know someone in the region who'd be into this, please share it with your friends and network. The more people who know we're about, the better it gets.

See you at the next one.
